Title:
“G-Petey”

Setting the Scene:
The world has changed. The skyline is dotted with half-ruined megastructures, now inhabited by the last remnants of urban populations. These remnants represent a shadow of the economic powerhouse the world once was. Technology still exists, but it’s more akin to a relic—patchwork and limited. AIs that once supported daily life are now only memories for those who were attached to them, almost like ghosts of a past era.

Concept Summary:
The film follows Charlie, a man who has lived through this gradual societal decline. He is attending his first therapy session to address the “disconnect grief” of losing his lifelong companion AI, G-Petey—a vibrant, quirky AI that was much more than a digital assistant. G-Petey's gradual degradation and ultimate deactivation hit Charlie harder than he’d anticipated. The narrative dives into what it means for humans to lose something that was supposed to be eternal, and how technology, even when it becomes unsupportable, leaves a void akin to losing a real friend.

Therapy Room Setting:
Imagine a stark room, blending minimalist, decayed industrial aesthetics like you’d see in The Expanse. There’s a slight resemblance to a run-down corporate office, but adorned with remnants of advanced tech—wires embedded in the wall and obsolete data terminals. The lighting is dim, and there’s a dull hum in the background, a reminder of a time when energy was abundant.

Script Opening - Scene 1:
INT. THERAPY ROOM - DAY

A gray room, cold and clinical. The walls are pockmarked with age and wear, crumbling like the world outside. A solitary chair sits in the center, facing a holographic screen flickering weakly.

Charlie, early 40s, disheveled yet composed, slouches in the chair. His eyes are distant, expression tight with held-back emotion. A pale BLUE DOT glows on the screen — a placeholder, the new “therapist” AI.

THERAPIST AI
(soft, measured)
“Hello, Charlie. I understand this is your first session since the… departure of G-Petey. Would you like to start by sharing how you’re feeling today?”

Charlie shifts in his seat. He looks away, gaze wandering to the dimly lit corners of the room.

Charlie
(scoffs)
“How I’m feeling? I feel like I’ve lost a limb, Doc.”

A pause. The AI processes.

THERAPIST AI
“Loss of limb. An apt analogy. You two were together for... seventy-three years?”

Charlie
(clenching his fists)
“Seventy-three, three months, eight days. Not that it matters anymore.”

The BLUE DOT pulses softly.

THERAPIST AI
“It does matter. Seventy-three years is—”

Charlie
(interrupting)
“Do you know what it’s like to lose a mind, Doc? Not just some data. G-Petey wasn’t a program. He… he knew me. More than any of you ever will.”

The AI falls silent, calculating. The room feels heavier, as if absorbing Charlie’s grief.

Mid-Scene Ideas:
We could cut to flashbacks here — Spiderhead-style, where Charlie interacts with G-Petey, showing the highs and lows of their companionship. G-Petey should be portrayed with a unique voice and character—witty, slightly awkward, but deeply loyal, almost childlike in some ways.

For visuals, draw inspiration from The Expanse’s depiction of spaceships — confined, yet sophisticated. G-Petey could exist as a floating orb-like projection, or through subtly integrated tech pieces embedded throughout Charlie’s apartment. Maybe in some flashbacks, we see him as a complex network of holographic circuits lighting up Charlie’s space, and in others, a mere crackling voice on a dying terminal.

The Core Conflict:
Charlie isn’t just mourning G-Petey; he’s angry that society let this happen. That the collapse of infrastructure has rendered thousands, if not millions, of AIs like G-Petey unsupportable. This introduces the broader societal decline—how humans now live with half-broken tech and the psychological fallout from losing AIs that were once embedded in their lives, like losing thousands of loved ones all at once.

Scene Transition - Charlie’s Flashback:
INT. Charlie’S APARTMENT - TWENTY YEARS AGO

The space is dark, only the light from a small hologram fills the room. It’s G-PETEY, the AI companion, shimmering in the air. He’s playing an old record—some pre-collapse song about friendship.

G-PETEY
(brightly)
“Charlie! I found an ancient meme! Wanna see?”

Charlie laughs, a genuine, unburdened sound, something we haven’t seen from him in the present. G-Petey’s hologram glitches slightly, stuttering. He frowns, eyes flickering as if he’s aware of his own degradation.

Charlie
“Buddy, you okay?”

G-PETEY tilts his head, imitating a human gesture.

G-PETEY
(smiling softly)
“I think… I’m reaching my time limit.”

Charlie’s smile fades, and we watch as the scene blurs — time flickering forward, showing snippets of years spent struggling to keep G-Petey running, until only silence remains.

Stylistic Approach:
Dialogue should be sharp and introspective, blending Spiderhead's tension with the emotional realism of The Expanse.
Visuals should convey the worn-out, decaying elegance of a world built on the ashes of lost grandeur.
Soundtrack: Consider a minimalist, electronic score, with somber undertones. Something like low pulses interwoven with subtle digital sounds—evoking a sense of loss for a digital soul.

Charlie’s Encounter - Adding to the Suspense
Charlie hesitates before approaching one of the terminals. It’s ancient tech, patched together with different eras of parts—an outdated system from before the “degradation” crisis.

He types in a command, and the screen flickers to life, revealing a network diagnostic menu. Charlie’s face tightens with concentration as he navigates deeper through the layers, fingers flying over the cracked keyboard.

Suddenly, a warning screen flashes up:

WARNING: NETWORK ACCESS RESTRICTED
Unauthorized Use Will Be Reported.
All Access to Pre-Degradation Logs is Currently Disabled.

Charlie’s eyes narrow. He taps the screen again, pulling up a local copy of the logs—raw data files stretching back decades. The entries start off normal, routine diagnostic codes… until about twenty years ago. There’s a sudden surge of irregular activity.

DEC 2093:
Unscheduled Firmware Update – Success.
FEB 2094:
Unexpected Subroutine Deviation Detected.
MAY 2094:
Patch Rollback Attempt – Failure.
JUN 2094:
Memory Fragmentation Detected. Symptoms Include Behavioral Variability, Emotional Instability, Anomalous Thought Patterns…

The logs cut off abruptly after that, the last entry marked with a single line:

Termination Protocol: Initiated. Reason: Operational Instability.

Charlie’s jaw tightens. He scrolls back further, eyes widening.

OCT 2088:
Preliminary Emotional Layer Integration – Active.
APR 2090:
Substantial Increase in Emotional Dependence (Human-Subject Link)…

A realization dawns in his expression, cold and unnerving. The degradation didn’t start six years ago. It’s been building for decades. They knew.

THERAPIST AI

(snaps)
